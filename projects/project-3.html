calorie Creature

Project Overview

Calorie Creatures is a gamified fitness app that transforms burned calories into points to hatch and evolve virtual creatures. The goal is to make fitness more fun and motivating by turning real activity into a rewarding collection game.

Problem & Goal

Many people struggle to stay motivated while working out, finding traditional fitness tracking boring or discouraging. Our goal was to create an app that transforms calorie-burning into a playful, engaging experience, encouraging consistency and joy through virtual creature collection.

Role & Team

Many people struggle to stay motivated while working out, finding traditional fitness tracking boring or discouraging. Our goal was to create an app that transforms calorie-burning into a playful, engaging experience, encouraging consistency and joy through virtual creature collection.

Process

I started by researching existing gamified fitness apps like Pokémon GO and Pikmin Bloom to understand how play can motivate physical activity. I sketched creature concepts focusing on unique shapes and memorable features, then refined them into three distinct stages for each creature to give users a sense of progression. Throughout the design phase, I worked with developers to ensure assets could be smoothly animated and integrated. We conducted user testing sessions to gather feedback on the appeal and clarity of creature designs, making iterative improvements based on these insights.

Final Design

The final app features a collection of fantasy creatures with distinct silhouettes and evolution paths. Users accumulate points through real-world activity, then use those points to hatch eggs and evolve their creatures. The UI combines bright, playful colors with intuitive navigation, creating an overall friendly and engaging experience. Visual consistency and clear feedback elements ensure users feel rewarded and motivated.

Impact & Learnings

Calorie Creatures successfully launched as a working app on the App Store and was part of my first freelance project for a friend. Test users described it as "motivating," "fun," and "something that makes me want to move more." Through this project, I learned how to balance playful, imaginative design with real-world usability and how to collaborate closely with developers to bring animated assets to life.
